01 / CASE STUDY

The research challenge

High-energy astronomical observations contain complex, noisy variability. Identifying candidate timing signatures requires careful treatment of measurement noise, instrumental constraints and statistical uncertainty.

The research challenge is to assess which timing features are statistically supported, accounting for stochastic variability, measurement noise and model assumptions.

05 / CASE STUDY

Transferable lessons

Research conclusions should remain specific to their datasets and assumptions. The same discipline is useful in industrial ML: validate data provenance, use appropriate error models, measure performance and separate hypotheses from demonstrated findings.

Research lesson

Apparent features in noisy time series require explicit statistical assumptions, careful significance assessment and uncertainty-aware interpretation.
